About Us

Your integrated partner for fasteners, engineered components and production-focused supply.

YOUR PRODUCTION. OUR COMMITMENT.

Built on expertise.
Driven by your progress.

Manufacturing works best when every part of the process works together. That is the principle behind Schrauben Technologies.

Since 2020, we have grown into an industrial distributor and service partner specializing in fasteners, precision machined components and integrated supply chain solutions. Our role is to bridge the gap between production requirements and dependable, cost-effective supply.

We support international manufacturers with supplier selection, planning, logistics and point-of-use inventory. By bringing these capabilities together, we help simplify procurement, reduce lead times, control costs and improve productivity.

OUR JOURNEY

From a clear vision
to a stronger operation.

Growing our people, capabilities and infrastructure around the needs of manufacturers.

2020

The foundation

A small team and a clear vision. Our journey began with a commitment to quality fasteners, learning and customer trust.

2021

Building confidence

Our first bulk order marked an early milestone. The team grew as we developed product quality and production processes.

2022

Our first plant

We established our first plant, expanded into industrial-grade fasteners, built in-house quality control and grew beyond 25 team members.

2023

Expanding capability

Operations scaled, key processes became automated, and the team grew to more than 60 people as we reached new production milestones.

2024

A second plant

A second manufacturing plant expanded our infrastructure. The team crossed 100 professionals and we developed customized fastening solutions.

2025

Moving forward

Two full-fledged plants and a team of 100+ professionals, with a continued focus on innovation, exports and sustainability.
