Screws

Standard and application-specific fastening solutions, supported by technical expertise and dependable supply.

A screw is an externally threaded part used with two or more components, at least one of which contains its own internal thread, which even may be formed by the installation of the screw itself. We can supply various types of cold/hot forged metric/Inch series standard screws with different shape, size, material and finish.

Coating options

  • Natural(Plain)
  • Rust Preventive Oiled
  • Zinc Plating with Trivalent
  • Clear/Black/Yellow/Blue Chromate Passivation
  • Zinc Plating with Olive Drab/Yellow Chromate Passivation
  • Zinc Iron Plated (Black)
  • Zinc Aluminum Flake Coating
  • Black Oxide Coating

Types of Screw

  • Drywall Screws
  • Chipboard Screws
  • Stainless Steel Screws
  • Wood Screws
  • Self Tapping Screws
  • Self Drilling Screws
